# Aura art

The user's AI aura pictures, cut out of their white background and saved at 512x512.
The glow is un-mixed from white into soft alpha (it keeps a channel near 255, the grey
body never does), so it reads on any card colour. Uploaded with the Studio MCP
`upload_image`; the ids are in `AuraLook.IMAGES`, which `client/GearArt` shows in the
Aura shop cards in place of the live atom.
